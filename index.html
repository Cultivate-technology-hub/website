    Products

    Cultivate Flow: AI-Powered Trading Operations Platform for Every Transaction

    Cultivate Technology delivers a comprehensive suite of Microsoft-based products built to modernise how trading organisations operate. Cultivate Flow is its flagship offering: an AI-powered trading operations platform that unifies transactions, contracts, documents, workflows, operations, logistics and finance into a single secure environment.

    Built natively on the Microsoft ecosystem and enhanced with AI throughout, Cultivate Flow helps CTRM (Commodity Trading and Risk Management) and ETRM (Energy Trading and Risk Management) teams move away from fragmented, manual processes toward connected, intelligent operations, with full auditability, governance, and enterprise-grade security built in from day one.

    The Challenge

    Most trading organisations, even sophisticated ones, still operate with a patchwork of tools that were never designed to work together:

    Collectively, this fragmentation drives up operational risk, slows decision-making, makes audits and compliance harder, and puts a hard ceiling on how much a business can scale without adding headcount at the same rate as transaction volume.

    The Answer

    Cultivate Flow replaces that patchwork with one connected operating platform covering the full transaction lifecycle, from creation through documentation, operations, logistics, finance, and settlement. CTRM and ETRM teams get a single place to originate, manage, monitor, and close out every transaction, with every document, task, counterparty, and financial event linked back to its source deal. This gives teams a consistent, real-time view of operational status and exposes exceptions before they become costly problems.
